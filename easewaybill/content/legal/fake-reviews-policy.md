# EaseWaybill Fake Reviews & User Content Policy

**Effective Date:** 14-09-2026
**Last Updated:** 14-09-2026

## 1. Introduction

This Policy sets out rules for reviews, ratings, and other user-generated content submitted on EaseWaybill, to keep information on the Platform honest and useful for buyers and sellers.

## 2. Prohibition on Fake Reviews

Users must not submit reviews for orders or sellers that did not actually occur, or that misrepresent their own experience. This includes reviews written by a seller (or someone acting on a seller's behalf) about their own listings, or by a buyer who did not actually transact with the seller in question.

## 3. Manipulated Ratings

Users must not artificially inflate or deflate ratings, including through the use of multiple accounts, coordinated review campaigns, or automated tools.

## 4. Paid or Incentivised Reviews

If a review was written in exchange for payment, discount, free goods, or any other incentive, this must be clearly and prominently disclosed within the review. Undisclosed incentivised reviews are prohibited.

## 5. Impersonation

Users must not impersonate another person, business, buyer, seller, or rider, or misrepresent their affiliation with EaseWaybill.

## 6. Fraudulent Claims

Users must not submit false claims in reviews, disputes, or support requests (e.g., falsely claiming non-delivery, damage, or misrepresentation of goods) in order to obtain a refund or damage a seller's reputation.

## 7. Review Moderation

EaseWaybill may moderate reviews and other user content, including removing content that violates this Policy, contains abusive language, discloses private information about another user, or is otherwise unlawful.

## 8. Removal of Prohibited Content

Where content is found to violate this Policy, EaseWaybill may remove or hide it, and may take account-level action against the user responsible (see Terms & Conditions, Sections 14–16, on prohibited activities and account suspension).
