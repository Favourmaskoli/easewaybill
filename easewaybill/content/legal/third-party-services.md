## 1. Introduction

EaseWaybill integrates certain third-party services to provide core functionality. This page explains which categories of third-party services we use, what happens when you interact with them, and how their policies relate to ours.

## 2. Categories of Third-Party Services We May Use

- **Payment providers** — currently **Paystack**, used to process payments and support our escrow-style payment-holding feature.
- **Maps / location services** — used, where applicable, to support delivery address input and rider tracking.
- **Analytics providers** — used, where enabled, to understand aggregate Platform usage. **[Confirm specific provider(s) before publishing.]**
- **Email providers** — used to send verification, transactional, and account-related emails.
- **Authentication / security services** — used to support secure login and fraud detection.
- **Embedded media or other external content** — where applicable (e.g., embedded help/support widgets).

## 3. What Happens When You Interact With Third-Party Services

When you make a payment, view a map, or interact with another embedded third-party feature, you may be interacting directly with that third party's systems, subject to that third party's own terms of service and privacy policy — not only ours. For example, when you enter payment details at checkout, you are providing them to Paystack (or the applicable processor) under Paystack's own security and privacy practices, not directly to EaseWaybill.

## 4. Third Parties Have Their Own Policies

We encourage you to review the terms and privacy policies of any third-party service you interact with through the Platform, including:

- Paystack's terms of service and privacy policy (for payment processing).
- Any analytics or email provider's applicable policies.

EaseWaybill is not responsible for the practices of independent third parties, though we choose service providers we consider reputable and require them to handle data appropriately where we share it (see Privacy Policy, Section 8).
