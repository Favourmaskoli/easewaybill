## 1. Introduction

EaseWaybill ("EaseWaybill," "we," "us," or "our") operates a logistics and order-management platform that connects buyers, sellers, and riders, and provides escrow-style payment protection for orders placed through the platform (the "Platform" or "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to anyone who uses the Platform, including buyers, sellers, riders, and website visitors.

By using EaseWaybill, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use EaseWaybill, we may collect:

- **Account information:** full name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed/encrypted form), role on the Platform (buyer, seller, or rider), profile photo (if provided).
- **Contact information:** delivery address, pickup address, alternative phone numbers.
- **Order information:** items/goods description, order value, order status, waybill/tracking codes, timestamps, communication between buyer and seller relating to an order.
- **Payment information:** limited payment metadata such as transaction reference, payment status, and amount. EaseWaybill does not directly store full card numbers, card PINs, CVV, or bank account credentials — these are handled by our payment processor (currently Paystack) in accordance with its own security standards.
- **Delivery/tracking information:** rider location data during an active delivery (e.g., GPS checkpoints/scan events), delivery status updates, proof-of-delivery data (e.g., confirmation scans, photos, or signatures where applicable).
- **Dispute information:** details submitted when a buyer or seller raises a dispute, including messages, evidence, and any notes generated during dispute review.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, cookies and similar identifiers, pages visited, and general usage/log data.
- **Communications:** messages you send to our support team, and in-app messages between buyers and sellers relating to an order.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal data (such as health, religious, or biometric data) and ask that you do not include such information in messages, disputes, or support requests.

## 3. How We Collect Data

We collect personal data:

- Directly from you, when you register an account, place or fulfil an order, message another user, contact support, or raise a dispute.
- Automatically, through cookies, log files, and similar technologies as you use the Platform (see our Cookie Policy).
- From riders' devices, during active deliveries, for the purpose of location tracking and checkpoint scanning.
- From third parties, such as our payment processor (Paystack), who share transaction status and limited metadata with us so we can update order and escrow status.

## 4. Why We Collect Data (Purposes)

We use personal data to:

1. Create and manage user accounts.
2. Process and track orders between buyers and sellers.
3. Facilitate escrow-style payment holding and release between buyers and sellers.
4. Coordinate deliveries and provide real-time or checkpoint-based tracking.
5. Enable communication between buyers, sellers, and riders regarding an order.
6. Investigate and help resolve disputes.
7. Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
8. Provide customer support.
9. Improve and maintain the Platform (bug fixing, analytics, performance monitoring).
10. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
11. Send transactional notifications (e.g., order updates, escrow release notices) and, where you have consented, other communications.

## 5. How We Use Payment-Related Information

- Payments made on EaseWaybill are processed by third-party payment processors, currently **Paystack**.
- EaseWaybill holds funds in an **escrow-style arrangement**: funds paid by a buyer for an order are held before being released to the seller, subject to the conditions described in our Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy.
- EaseWaybill is **not a bank, licensed payment institution, or licensed escrow provider** unless and until such status is explicitly confirmed and disclosed. Please see Section 8 ("Local Laws & Regulatory Compliance" document) regarding the regulatory status of fund-holding arrangements — this is an item requiring confirmation from a Nigerian lawyer/compliance professional before public launch.
- We receive and store limited payment metadata (transaction reference, status, amount, timestamp) to reconcile orders and escrow status. We do not store full card details; these are handled by Paystack under its own security and privacy practices.

## 6. Delivery/Tracking Information

- Riders' location may be collected during an active delivery to provide buyers and sellers with tracking updates and to verify checkpoint scans.
- Location data collected from riders is used only for delivery coordination, tracking display, dispute investigation, and safety/fraud purposes, and is retained only as long as necessary for those purposes (see Section 10).
- Buyers and sellers can view tracking information relevant to their own orders. We do not share a rider's location with parties unrelated to the specific delivery.

## 7. Cookies and Analytics

EaseWaybill uses cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, essential platform functions, and (where enabled) analytics. Full details, including how to manage your preferences, are set out in our separate **Cookie Policy**.

## 8. Data Sharing with Service Providers

We may share personal data with trusted third parties who help us operate the Platform, including:

- **Payment processors** (currently Paystack), to process payments and facilitate escrow holding/release.
- **Email/communication providers**, to send transactional and account-related emails (e.g., verification, order updates, escrow notices).
- **Cloud hosting and database providers**, to store and process Platform data securely.
- **Analytics providers** (if enabled), to help us understand usage and improve the Platform.
- **Riders/logistics partners**, to the extent necessary to fulfil deliveries (e.g., sharing pickup/delivery address and contact details relevant to a specific order).

We require service providers to handle personal data only for the purposes we specify and in a manner consistent with this Policy. We do **not** sell personal data to third parties.

We may also disclose data where required by law, regulation, court order, or valid request from a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of EaseWaybill, our users, or the public.
