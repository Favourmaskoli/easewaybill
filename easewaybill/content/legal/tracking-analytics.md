## 1. Introduction

This Disclosure explains the technical and behavioural information EaseWaybill may collect when you use the Platform, separate from the personal data covered directly in the Privacy Policy. It should be read alongside the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

## 2. What Technical Information May Be Collected

### 2.1 IP Addresses

We may log IP addresses to secure accounts, detect fraud/abuse, and understand general geographic usage patterns.

### 2.2 Device / Browser Information

We may collect device type, operating system, browser type/version, and screen size to ensure the Platform displays and functions correctly.

### 2.3 Usage / Activity Data

We may log actions such as pages viewed, features used, order actions taken, and time spent on the Platform, to understand usage and improve the Service.

### 2.4 Security Logging

We log certain events (e.g., login attempts, password changes, unusual account activity) to detect and respond to security threats.

### 2.5 Analytics

Where enabled, we may use analytics tools to understand aggregate usage trends. **[Confirm specific analytics tool(s) in use before publishing, e.g., Google Analytics or an equivalent.]**

### 2.6 Performance Monitoring

We may collect technical performance data (e.g., load times, error logs) to identify and fix issues.

### 2.7 Fraud / Security Monitoring

We may analyse account and transaction patterns to detect suspicious activity, consistent with our obligations to protect users and the integrity of the escrow system.

## 3. How Tracking Relates to Cookies

Some of the technical data above is collected via cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy for details on cookie categories and how to manage them.

## 4. How You Can Control Optional Tracking

You can:

- Manage optional cookie categories (e.g., analytics) via the cookie-consent banner or preference centre described in our Cookie Policy;
- Adjust your browser or device settings to limit tracking generally;
- Contact us with questions about specific tracking practices.

Please note that some tracking (e.g., security logging, essential functionality) cannot be disabled, as it is necessary for the Platform to operate safely.
