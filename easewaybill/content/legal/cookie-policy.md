## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences, and, where enabled, understand how the site is used. "Similar technologies" (e.g., local storage, pixels) are covered by this Policy in the same way as cookies.

## 2. Categories of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies

Necessary for the Platform to function (e.g., loading pages, remembering items in a checkout flow). The Platform cannot work properly without these.

### 2.2 Authentication / Session Cookies

Used to keep you logged in, recognise your session, and secure your account while you use the Platform.

### 2.3 Security Cookies

Used to help detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access (e.g., recognising suspicious login patterns).

### 2.4 Analytics Cookies

Used, where enabled, to understand how users interact with the Platform (e.g., pages visited, general usage patterns) so we can improve it. **[Confirm actual analytics tool(s) in use, e.g., Google Analytics, before publishing.]**

### 2.5 Preference Cookies

Used to remember your settings and preferences (e.g., language, display options) for a better experience.

### 2.6 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third parties integrated into the Platform (e.g., our payment processor during checkout). These are governed by the relevant third party's own cookie/privacy policy.

## 4. Essential vs. Optional Cookies

**Essential cookies** (functionality, authentication, security) are required for the Platform to operate and cannot be switched off through our cookie settings, though you can block them via your browser (which may break parts of the Platform).

**Optional cookies** (analytics, preference, and some third-party cookies) are only set with your consent and can be withdrawn at any time.
