# EaseWaybill Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** 14-09-2026
**Last Updated:** 14-09-2026

## 1. Our Commitment

EaseWaybill is committed to making our Platform usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to follow recognised accessibility practices (informed by standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG) as our Platform develops, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing effort rather than a one-time task.

## 2. Keyboard Accessibility

We aim to ensure that key actions on the Platform (navigation, forms, buttons) can be operated using a keyboard alone, without requiring a mouse or touch input.

## 3. Screen-Reader Compatibility

We aim to use semantic HTML and appropriate labelling (e.g., alt text for images, ARIA attributes where needed) so that screen-reader users can understand and navigate the Platform.

## 4. Colour and Contrast

We aim to use colour combinations with sufficient contrast between text and background, and to avoid relying on colour alone to convey important information (e.g., order status, error states).

## 5. Responsive / Mobile Accessibility

The Platform is designed to be usable across a range of screen sizes and devices, including mobile phones commonly used by riders and users in the field.

## 6. Accessible Forms and Error Messages

We aim to label form fields clearly, associate error messages with the relevant field, and describe errors in plain language so users know what needs to be corrected (e.g., during account registration, order creation, or payment).

## 7. Known Limitations

As an actively developed product, some parts of the Platform may not yet fully meet all accessibility best practices. We are working to improve this over time.
