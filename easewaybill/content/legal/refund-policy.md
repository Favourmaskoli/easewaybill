## 1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds may be available for orders placed on EaseWaybill. It works together with our Terms & Conditions and should be read alongside them. It applies to buyers and sellers using the Platform's escrow-style payment feature.

## 2. How EaseWaybill's Escrow-Style Payment Works

When a buyer pays for an order, funds are held by EaseWaybill in an escrow-style arrangement rather than being released to the seller immediately. Funds are released to the seller when:

- The buyer confirms satisfactory receipt of the order; or
- A fixed holding period elapses without a dispute being raised (currently an automatic release window — see Terms & Conditions for the applicable duration); or
- A dispute is resolved in the seller's favour.

Refunds to the buyer are only possible **before** funds have been released to the seller, or where a dispute is resolved in the buyer's favour and reversal is technically possible through our payment processor.

## 3. When a Buyer Can Request a Refund

A buyer may request a refund where, for example:

- The order was not delivered within the agreed or reasonable timeframe;
- The seller cancelled the order after payment;
- The item(s) delivered were materially different from what was ordered (e.g., wrong item, significantly not as described);
- The item(s) arrived damaged or defective, where this is not attributable to the buyer or the delivery process outside the seller's control;
- Both buyer and seller agree to cancel the order before dispatch.

## 5. Failed, Cancelled, or Disputed Orders

- **Failed delivery attempts:** If delivery fails for reasons attributable to the seller or rider (e.g., wrong address supplied by the seller, non-availability of the seller), the buyer may be entitled to a refund or re-delivery, as agreed between the parties or determined through dispute resolution.
- **Cancelled orders:** If an order is cancelled before dispatch by mutual agreement, funds held in escrow will generally be refunded to the buyer, less any non-recoverable processing fees (see Section 9).
- **Disputed orders:** Where a dispute is raised, escrow release is paused pending review. Outcomes may include full refund, partial refund, release to the seller, or another resolution agreed by the parties or determined by EaseWaybill's dispute process (see Terms & Conditions, including provisions on AI-assisted dispute analysis).

## 6. Escrow / Payment-Release Implications

- Raising a dispute pauses the automatic release timer for that order's escrow funds.
- Once funds have been released to a seller (either automatically or following confirmation), reversing that release may not be possible, and the buyer's recourse may be limited to a direct arrangement with the seller or, where applicable, other legal remedies.
- EaseWaybill will make reasonable efforts to prevent premature release where a dispute is raised in time, but cannot guarantee reversal of a payment that has already settled with the seller through the payment processor.

## 8. Situations Where Refunds May Not Be Available

Refunds may not be available where, for example:

- The buyer confirmed receipt of the order and no dispute was raised within the applicable window;
- The issue arises from the buyer's own error (e.g., wrong address or item selection) rather than the seller's or rider's conduct;
- The claim is submitted after the applicable dispute window has closed;
- The order falls into a category the seller has clearly marked as non-refundable (where such a term is lawful and not misleading), consistent with applicable consumer-protection law;
- The payment processor is unable to reverse the transaction (e.g., because funds have already settled and cannot technically be recalled).

Nothing in this Policy is intended to override any refund or remedy right you may have under applicable Nigerian consumer-protection law (including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act, 2018) that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Any transaction or processing fees charged by Paystack that are non-recoverable will be disclosed where possible before you complete a refund request.

## 10. Responsibilities of Buyers and Sellers

- **Buyers** are responsible for providing accurate delivery information, inspecting orders promptly, and raising disputes within the applicable window.
- **Sellers** are responsible for accurately describing items, dispatching orders promptly, and responding to disputes in good faith and within any required timeframe.
- Both parties are expected to communicate through the Platform and cooperate with any dispute review process.

## 11. How Disputes Affect Refunds

Raising a dispute through the Platform pauses escrow release and triggers our dispute-resolution process (described in the Terms & Conditions). The outcome of a dispute determines whether a refund, partial refund, or release to the seller occurs. Dispute decisions may involve AI-assisted analysis of the evidence submitted, but final outcomes remain subject to human review and are not automated legal determinations.
